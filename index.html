Candy Calc Team Builder

Fill up to six party slots, set where each Pokemon is and where you want it, and get the cheapest pile of Exp. Candy that gets everyone there.

What to feed them

Exp. Candy yields: XS 100, S 800, M 3,000, L 10,000, XL 30,000. At Quasartico Inc. an S costs 8 Mega Shards, an M costs 30 and an L costs 100 — a flat 100 Exp. per shard whichever size you buy, so the only way to save shards is to waste less Exp.
